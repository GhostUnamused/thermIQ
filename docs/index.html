How gaps are scored

A gap is a topic the CEA guideline baseline expects a plant to document, where this plant's own documents give weak or no coverage. Risk = criticality (1–5, sourced) × consequence (₹ Cr, from CEA forced-outage records at ₹5.0/kWh) × exposure (1 − best match in the plant's documents). Only topics with real CEA outage history for their equipment are priced here; the rest are listed under "Needs documentation" without a guessed figure.
